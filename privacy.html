Privacy Policy

Documentary Studio — last updated 10 November 2026

Documentary Studio ("the application") is a desktop application that runs on its author's own Windows computer. It has a single user: its author. It is not offered as a service, it is not distributed, and it has no accounts, no sign-ups and no server of its own.

This policy explains what the application does with information from your Google account when you sign in to it.

Information the application accesses

When you sign in with Google, the application requests these permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — to upload videos to your YouTube channel and to set their thumbnails.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl — to post a comment under a video you uploaded, and to add that video to one of your own playlists.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly — to read the YouTube Analytics reports of your own channel.

Each permission serves one purpose, and only for the channel of the person who signs in:

  • Uploading. A video file you produced with the application is uploaded to your channel, with the title, description, tags, thumbnail and publication date you chose.
  • Commenting. A comment whose text you wrote yourself is posted under that video. The application shows you the comment before the upload, and posts it only when you start that upload. It never comments on anyone else's video, and never likes, replies, moderates or deletes anything.
  • Playlists. The uploaded video is added to a playlist of your own channel that you chose.
  • Analytics. Reports on your own videos — such as audience retention and average view duration — are read so that you can judge how long future videos should be. They are shown only to you.

The application does not request access to your email, your contacts, your files, your profile details, your viewing history, your subscribers' identities, or any other Google service.

Information the application stores

After you sign in, Google issues an authorization token. The application stores that token in a single folder on your own computer:

  • %LOCALAPPDATA%\DocumentaryStudio\

The token stays on that computer. It exists so that you do not have to sign in again before every upload. It is never uploaded, synchronised, backed up to a remote service, or transmitted anywhere except to Google's own servers when the application refreshes it.

YouTube Analytics reports the application reads are saved on the same computer, in the application's own folders, and nowhere else. They are kept only while the application is still authorised: the application checks that authorisation at least every 30 days, and deletes the reports when it is no longer granted.

The application does not keep a copy of the comments it posts or of your playlists; those live on YouTube.

What the application does not do

  • It has no server, no database and no hosted component. There is nowhere for your data to be sent.
  • It does not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anybody.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or cookies. This website does not either — it is static HTML with no scripts and no third-party resources.
  • It does not access, collect or process the YouTube data of any person other than the person signed in. It does not read other people's comments or channels.
  • It does not combine your Analytics data with anyone else's, or derive new metrics from it presented as YouTube's own.
  • It does not use your Google data to train any machine-learning or artificial intelligence model.

Limited use of Google user data

Documentary Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The application uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, post a comment under them, add them to a playlist, and read your channel's Analytics reports. By using it, you are also agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information collected and handled by Google is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting your data

You are in control of this at all times, and you do not need to ask anyone:

  • To revoke the application's access, visit Google security settings, select Documentary Studio, and choose "Remove access". The stored token stops working immediately, and the Analytics reports the application saved are deleted within 30 days.
  • To delete what the application stores, delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\DocumentaryStudio\ and the saved Analytics reports. A deletion you ask for is carried out within 7 days. There is no copy anywhere else.
  • Comments and playlist entries the application created on your channel are yours, and can be edited or deleted in YouTube Studio like any other.

Children

The application is a personal production tool and is not directed at children. It has no users other than its author.
